# HUEGO — Value Proposition Canvas v0.1

## 1. Customer Segment

Primary:
- Independent travelers visiting Huế.
- Young travelers, couples, and friend groups.
- Travelers with limited time who want a practical plan.

## 2. Customer Jobs

Functional jobs:
- Find places to visit.
- Decide where to eat.
- Plan a day-by-day schedule.
- Estimate travel time.
- Control spending.
- Adapt plans when preferences change.

Emotional jobs:
- Feel confident about the trip.
- Avoid wasting time.
- Discover authentic local experiences.

## 3. Customer Pains

- Information is scattered.
- Too much time spent researching.
- Difficult to order destinations efficiently.
- Generic recommendations.
- Risk of outdated or inaccurate information.
- Hard to balance budget and interests.
- Local places can be difficult to discover.

## 4. Customer Gains

- A ready-to-use itinerary.
- Less planning effort.
- Better route efficiency.
- Personalized recommendations.
- Budget visibility.
- Local Huế experiences.
- Easy re-planning.

## 5. Products & Services

HUEGO provides:
- AI trip planner.
- Huế destination database.
- Route-aware itinerary.
- Budget estimation.
- Explore.
- Place Detail.
- HUEGO NOW.
- Saved trips.

## 6. Pain Relievers

HUEGO:
- Consolidates destination information.
- Generates an itinerary instead of only listing places.
- Uses coordinates and routing to improve ordering.
- Uses user preferences.
- Provides manual location fallback.
- Uses structured destination data to reduce AI hallucination risk.

## 7. Gain Creators

HUEGO creates value through:
- Personalized schedules.
- Contextual recommendations.
- Local destination knowledge.
- Flexible re-planning.
- Nearby recommendations.
- A focused Huế-first experience.

## 8. Core Value Proposition

> HUEGO helps independent travelers turn their limited time, budget, and personal interests into a practical Huế itinerary — without spending hours piecing information together from different sources.

## 9. Key Differentiation

HUEGO is not simply:
- A chatbot.
- A map.
- A list of tourist attractions.

HUEGO combines:
`Local Huế Data + AI Planning + Route Optimization + Personalization`

This combination is the central product proposition.
